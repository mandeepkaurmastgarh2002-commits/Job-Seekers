How it works

  1. Search. Pick your job and city. Open the real job sites in one tap.
  2. Prepare. Practise the questions that candidates really get asked.
  3. Show your skill. A 30 second video says more than a long resume.

Job searches and the resume checker run on your phone. Nothing you type there is saved by us. Your step ticks stay on your phone. Work Path Go needs an account, because bosses and workers must be able to trust each other.

Questions

Is First Job Helper really free?

The job search, the resume checker, the practice timer and Work Path Go are free. Extra packs with full model answers and resume templates cost a small price (from Rs 10). They are coming soon.

Do you save my resume?

No. The resume checker runs inside your browser. What you type is not sent to any server and is gone when you close the page.

Does it show real jobs?

It opens the real search pages of LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed and Google with your job and city filled in. The jobs you see come from those websites, not from here.

Will these tools get me a job?

No tool can promise that. They help you search faster, fix missing words in your resume and practise answers. Your results depend on how many good applications you send and how you do in interviews.
